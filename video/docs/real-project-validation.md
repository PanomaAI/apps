# Real-project validation

The September 2026 round exercises actual installed products through discovery,
recording, editorial planning, scene revisions and encoded export. Fixtures remain
the regression suite; they do not count as real products. Working material and the
per-export reports live under `PANOMA_VIDEO_HOME/validation/five-products-2026-09-05`.

## Coverage

| Product | What the recording may establish | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Panoma | Local project catalog and the visible list of work without backup | A backup or code publication completed |
| SUNDECK web prototype (old DesignContext checkout) | Its built-in comparison of design previews with and without context | The separate native application's features or a live AI generation |
| MenuCard, built-in Demo Mode | Opening its printing catalog and viewing available choices | A printed order, payment, sale or AI conversation completed |
| Universend | Selecting celestial destinations and the corresponding real 3D camera movement | Sending a signal, reacting or replying to someone |
| Ledgerly Games | Selecting a historical scenario, bias and thesis to prepare a browser-local training forecast | A revealed or graded result, real trades, investment performance or real user achievements |

The original projects are read, never installed into or used as writable capture
directories. Existing Panoma is filmed through its supplied instance; other dev
servers run from disposable copies with their installed dependencies. Demo state
initialized by the application belongs to that isolated runtime.

## Defects the round exercises

- A semantic scroll must reveal a control through its clipping ancestors in both
  layouts, then measure the actual pointer position and press-time macro.
- Responsive decoration may change an accessible name. An alternate identity must
  resolve to the exact unique element on both layouts, or the action is omitted.
- Explicit visual selection state can change without an accessibility label change.
  Such observations are separate from labels and never invent an outcome sentence.
- Repository boilerplate is not the final product thesis. Bounded, redacted live
  context precedes the tour; recorded states refine the argument afterward. Both
  calls use the existing schema, cache and call cap.
- Product identity carries a source. An explicit correction resolves the SUNDECK
  header versus old DesignContext document title; it changes the film only.
- Offline edits retain measured branding. A saved story's source is replaced only
  with `--new-story`; refusal must preserve facts, footage and an editable revision.
- Automatic split compositions retain the recorded result's CSS scale in every
  served format. This is a size floor, not a guarantee of legibility. A saved revision
  can retain explicit choices, while new automatic plans use full frame when needed.
- A prelaunch app without a genuine public address closes on its sourced brand.
  Provider setup links and local preview URLs cannot become a product destination.

On the same Apple Silicon host, Universend's software-rendered desktop take lasted
about 178 seconds; hardware Metal capture reduced it to about 17 seconds while
retaining 3840 × 2160 source pixels. Its first pointer approach fell from about 84
seconds to 1.2 seconds. This is a measured case, not a cross-platform benchmark.
The frame renderer retains its deterministic software rasterizer.

## Acceptance

Choose one hook in Spanish per product, render both 9:16 and 16:9 through the same
reviewed export path, and keep the revision, encoded review, provenance and post kit
beside each MP4. Inspect actual result frames and the encoded contact sheet; check
action timing, readable copy, truthful closing identity and the sound mix. A failed
review is corrected and exported again, never relabelled as a pass.

Passing these five examples does not certify arbitrary applications, every route,
all languages or viral performance. In particular, observed pixels establish a
visible change; they do not by themselves establish the business outcome an app
advertises. The final local validation report records the delivered files and the
specific limitations of each capture.
