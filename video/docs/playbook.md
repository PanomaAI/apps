# The virality playbook

Distilled from platform data (Buffer 1.1M TikToks; Socialinsider 69M videos,
2025-2026) — full citations in research.md. These numbers drive panoma video's defaults.

## Length — the sub-10s intuition is wrong

- TikToks over 60s got **43% more reach** than 30-60s, and **96% more than 5-10s**.
  Sub-10s clips are effectively reach-penalized.
- TikTok's own sweet spot: **21-34s**. Completion drops after 30s but the algorithm
  optimizes *total engaged watch time*, and rewatch weighs more than completion.
- Defaults: **TikTok/Reels 25-45s · Shorts 30-60s · X 30-60s · LinkedIn 30-60s**.
- The exception that earns its place: a **seamless loop under 10s** engineered for
  2-3 rewatches (terminal ASMR, before/after) — supplement, never core.

## Hooks — the first 2 seconds

- Never open with a logo or title card. Open **mid-action** (terminal already
  scrolling) with text appearing by ~0.5s.
- Formulas that fit a dev tool: negative ("Stop grepping your disk..."),
  count-shock ("You have 47 projects. Name ten."), POV pain ("POV: you need that
  side project from March"), before/after, speedrun-against-the-clock.

## Captions

- 75-85% watch muted. Word-by-word kinetic captions lift watch time 12-25%.
- Dense technical strings (commands, paths) go on screen as typography, not in the
  caption stream.

## One master file, uploaded natively everywhere

- No cross-platform duplicate penalty — the penalty is **watermarks** (40-60% reach
  loss for TikTok watermarks on Reels). panoma video renders clean masters; upload natively.
- Export: H.264 High, 1080x1920, 30fps, 10-14 Mbps, AAC 192-256k/48kHz, -14 LUFS.

## Cadence

- TikTok 3-5x/week, Reels 3-4x, Shorts 2-4x. Three strong videos beat seven mediocre.
- Every video ships from the matrix anyway — the marginal cost of the es version and
  the 1:1 crop is zero. Post them.

## What converts for dev tools (not just what reaches)

- Supabase found X memes out-convert TikTok reach; Raycast runs education on YouTube +
  tips on X; Fireship proved information density IS retention for dev audiences.
- panoma's three archetypes: build-in-public demo clips, folder-chaos POV humor,
  screen-demo-with-captions. The grandfather belongs to a fourth: story time.


> **On the length numbers in this file.** They came from vendor and SEO posts, and
> a 2026-09-01 sweep for primary sources could not stand any of them up: "TikTok's
> sweet spot is 21-34s", "under 10s is penalised", "Reels over 60s lose completion".
> What does survive is the CHI 2024 TikTok data donation (4.1M videos): 24% of views
> are gone before a fifth of the duration, 40% before half, and 45% reach the end —
> which argues about where the payload sits, not about how long the piece is.
> Treat every duration target below as a hypothesis.
